Frequently asked questions

What Tesla footage does CamCut support?

CamCut works with footage saved to a USB drive from any Tesla vehicle. Import the folder containing the TeslaCam or SavedClips sub-folders directly from your USB drive or Files app.

How do I restore CamCut Premium after reinstalling?

Open CamCut, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored on any device signed into the same account at no additional charge.

Why are some camera angles missing from my footage?

Tesla saves footage from the cameras that were active at the time of the event. Narrow, front, left, and right pillar cameras are recorded depending on your vehicle's hardware and the trigger type. Missing angles just means that camera wasn't recording for that clip.

Does CamCut upload my videos anywhere?

No. All processing happens entirely on your device. Your videos never leave your iPhone. See our Privacy Policy for details.

The export seems slow — is that normal?

Video export is GPU-intensive and render time depends on clip length, resolution, and number of overlays. Longer clips with multiple overlays will take longer to render. Keep your device plugged in during export for best performance.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email edgecase411@gmail.com with a description of what happened, your device model, iOS version, and if possible the steps to reproduce. Screenshots or screen recordings are extremely helpful.
