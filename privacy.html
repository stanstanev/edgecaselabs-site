Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 23, 2026  ·  Effective Date: September 23, 2026
Summary: CamCut processes your Tesla dashcam videos entirely on your device. We do not collect or store your videos, telemetry, location, device identifiers, or usage analytics. If you voluntarily join the CamCut Link notification list, we store the email address you provide solely to send you CamCut Link updates.

1. Introduction

CamCut is developed and operated by EdgeCase Labs LLC, a California limited liability company ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the CamCut mobile application (the "App").

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

2. Information We Collect and Do Not Collect

CamCut is built with a privacy-first approach. Except for the optional CamCut Link notification list described below, we do not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your Tesla dashcam videos or any video content
  • GPS coordinates, location data, or driving routes
  • Vehicle telemetry (speed, G-force, steering angle, gear, autopilot status)
  • Your name, phone number, physical address, or contacts
  • Your Apple ID or account credentials
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or analytics data
  • Usage patterns, screen views, or feature interactions
  • Crash logs or diagnostic reports
  • Photos, contacts, calendar, or other personal data outside the App

There is no CamCut account to create and nothing to log in to.

Optional CamCut Link notification list

If you choose to enter your email address and tap “Notify me” in the CamCut Link preview, we collect that email address so we can send you news about CamCut Link, an upcoming physical accessory. Joining the list is optional and does not unlock, restrict, or change any App functionality.

We store the email address together with the signup date and the version of CamCut used to join. This information is stored using Cloudflare services and is used only for CamCut Link communications. We do not sell it, use it for cross-company tracking, or share it with advertising networks or data brokers. The network address used for abuse prevention is not retained with the signup.

We retain this information until you opt out or the notification list is retired. You may opt out through the unsubscribe instructions in a CamCut Link email or by contacting edgecase411@gmail.com. We will remove your address from the list.

3. Local Processing

All video importing, editing, composition, overlay rendering, and export happens entirely on your iPhone. Your footage never leaves your device unless you explicitly share or export it using iOS's standard share sheet.

The App requires access to files you explicitly select (dashcam footage from USB or your device storage) and your Photo Library only if you choose to save exported videos there. We do not access any files you have not deliberately chosen to import.

4. Apple Frameworks

CamCut uses standard Apple frameworks including AVFoundation, MapKit, and the Photos framework. These frameworks may access Apple services (such as loading map tiles from Apple's servers when a map overlay is used). Any such access is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, not ours. We do not receive or store any data from these interactions.

5. In-App Purchases

CamCut offers an optional one-time purchase ("CamCut Premium") to unlock overlay features. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive only a cryptographic receipt to verify that a purchase has been made. We do not receive your payment method, billing address, Apple ID, or any financial information.

6. Children's Privacy

CamCut is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the App in a way that raises privacy concerns, please contact us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves. When material changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
