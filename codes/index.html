Apple Promo Code Rules

  • Each code works once.
  • Codes expire at 11:59 P.M. PT on the date shown in App Store Connect History.
  • Only give codes to people in regions where CamCut is available.
  • Every code must come with Apple's localized Holder Terms and its expiration date. That includes screenshots, printouts, and messages.
  • Codes can't be sold or exchanged for anything.
  • Don't use codes to give inappropriate content to minors.
  • People who redeem a promo code can't rate or review the app on the App Store.
  • Apple allows up to 100 codes per app version.

Erase All Codes?

This deletes every code, its status, the expiration date, and the terms from this phone. It can't be undone. Export Status first if you want a record.

Turn brightness all the way up

QR codes scan much faster on a bright screen. Swipe down from the top-right corner and drag brightness to max. This page can't change it for you.

Tip: turn off Auto-Lock in Settings → Display & Brightness if the screen dims during the event.
